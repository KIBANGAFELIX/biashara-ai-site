Data Deletion

Last updated: 3 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your Biashara AI account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to delete your account

The quickest way is in WhatsApp:

  1. From the number you use with Biashara AI, send FUTA AKAUNTI (or "delete my account").
  2. Biashara AI will explain what will be deleted and ask you to confirm.
  3. Reply FUTA SASA within 10 minutes. Your account and records are deleted straight away, and you get a confirmation message.

If you can't use WhatsApp, you can also ask us by email at info@biasharaai.co.ke. Include the WhatsApp number you use with Biashara AI. We may ask you to confirm the request from that number, so we know it really comes from you.

What we delete

  • Your account details, including your business name.
  • Your conversation history with the assistant.
  • Your business records: sales, expenses, budgets and balance sheet figures.

What we may keep

We may keep records of payments you made to us where the law requires us to keep them for tax or accounting purposes. We keep these only for as long as required.

Messages you exchanged with us may also remain on your own phone in WhatsApp. You can delete those yourself in the WhatsApp app.

Confirmation

When you delete through WhatsApp, you get a confirmation straight away. For email requests, we will confirm by email once your data has been deleted.
